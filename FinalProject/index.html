Welcome to Iron Barrel and Hide Leather Works

Why Choose Handmade Leather?

Handmade leather goods offer a personal touch that mass-produced products often do not have. Each piece of leather has its own marks, texture, and character, which makes every wallet unique.

The goal of Iron Barrel and Hide Leather Works is to create wallets that are practical, durable, and meaningful. Every product is made to feel one-of-a-kind.
